WHO WE ARE

Building Infrastructure with Precision & Integrity

DATSON CONSTRUCTION PLC was established in 2009 E.C. registered under the Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia, Oromia Region, Jimma Zone, Jimma City Government Finance and Economic Development Bureau.

Operating under construction license category GC-4, our firm undertakes all city work construction with the necessary expertise, high quality standards, and strict adherence to rules, safety regulations, and project monitoring systems.
